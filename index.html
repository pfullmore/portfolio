Cue 1 — About

Two disciplines, one instinct for how things should feel.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at UCF with hands-on experience in 3D modeling, design, FEA, and prototyping, and a less typical background as a professional stage performer. These two disciplines ask the same question in different languages: how do you make something complex feel effortless?

That question is what pulls me toward themed entertainment. I like projects where the technical constraint and the creative intent are based around the same problem.

This site acts as a portfolio, and it contains a handful of projects that show how I work: detail-oriented, collaborative, and concentrated on creating something meaningful.
